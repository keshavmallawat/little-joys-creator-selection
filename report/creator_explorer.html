Read this before you rely on it. The ad figures behind the report are simulated, not Little Joys results. The labels come from an AI system and are not always right. Rank stability and the weightings column are post-hoc checks, not part of the frozen score. This page shows associations, not proof that a creator will sell.

Score rank is the frozen pre-set score (1 = highest). Stability is the share of 200 post-resampling runs in which the creator is in the top five. "Weightings" counts, out of 4, how many score versions (the original and three alternates) put the creator in the top five. Food, sponsored and comment-for-link are shares of the creator's posts in the 12 months to 2026-10-27. The number of posts differs by creator (see the Posts column), so a percentage on a small count moves a lot with one post.
