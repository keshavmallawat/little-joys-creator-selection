# Decisions

- Audit treats a blank stopped_date as live on 2026-10-26; days_live checked against (stopped_date or 2026-10-26) minus launch_date.
- Audit reports ad counts at candidate spend floors [0, 1000, 5000, 10000, 25000, 50000, 100000]; the spend floor itself is not yet chosen.
- Audit flags posts with likes <= 5 as possible hidden like counts.
- Audit counts a transcript as present when the transcript cell is non-null and non-empty.
- Sponsored share reported against the is_sponsored breakdown (yes/no/unclear), since the field is categorical rather than boolean.
- days_live is used as recorded in the data; no analysis depends on exact day counts.
- Supplementary audit reports multi-value label columns both as literal whole-cell values and as atomic labels split on ';' (an ad can carry several atomic labels, so atomic counts may sum past the ad total).
- Supplementary audit flags a transcript as placeholder-looking by three facts: length under 100 characters, identical text repeated across posts, or a sentence of 15+ characters recurring 3 or more times within one transcript.
- Hypotheses, exposure floor (spend >= 10000, sensitivity 5000 and 25000) and minimum group size (10) fixed before any performance metric was computed.
- Cross-structure audit forms every hypothesis group straight from config/hypotheses.yaml, applying H2's child_on_screen != none restriction before its own filter; it computes no performance metric broken down by any group.
- Pairwise group overlap and per-group splits (by product_form, launch month, stopped_date) are formed at the primary floor only; group sizes alone are reported across all three floors.
- Pairwise overlap covers all 36 unordered pairs among the nine primary and exploratory hypotheses.
- Date-and-form clustering (distinct launch_date; top launch_date x stopped_date x product_form combinations) is reported over both all ads and the primary-floor set, side by side.
- Purchases min, median and count under 20 are reported for the primary-floor set as a whole, as a distribution and not as a per-group metric.
- Any hypothesis group under 10 ads at the chosen floor is dropped, no exceptions.
- The days_live offset follows stopped_date batches and is treated as a data artifact; ratio metrics do not use days_live.
- A spend floor partly selects ads the platform kept funding, so comparisons are among surviving ads.
- Outcome metric, robustness checks and verdict tiers fixed before any performance metric was computed; H8 evaluated within H6 because every H8 ad is a H6 ad; child-status three-way split added as descriptive.
- Label spot-check sample: 20 ads at the primary floor (spend >= 10000), seed 42, stratified so each of H1/H2/H4/H6/H7 has at least 3 ads in-group and 3 out-group where available; out-group is the floor set minus in-group, except H2 whose out-group keeps its child-present precondition (child_on_screen != none) and requires child_in_first_3s == 'no', so no-child ads do not pad that label's negatives.
- Label sheet shows each ad's video_url, hook_line and the first 600 characters of its transcript alongside the stored labels; reviewer judgments are collected in blank my_* columns; the sampler does not inspect or judge any label value.
- Label agreement scored per label with its n; my_child_role_reacts scored as presence of reacts_to_taste; reviewer entries of 'unclear' are excluded from n and the rate and tallied separately; the review sheet is read as utf-8-sig; the label_cap threshold is read from config/decision_rules.yaml and labels below it are flagged as a fact, with no winner chosen.
- Label spot-check done by hand on 20 ads: 114/120 cells agree (95%), no label below the 0.8 cap. Silent ads LJ-020 and LJ-091 account for 4 of the 6 mismatches (brand-said and creator-type definitions); both child-in-first-3s mismatches were the label saying yes where review said no.
- Performance pass computes pooled ROAS (sum revenue / sum spend) and diagnostic hook_rate/ctr/cvr per hypothesis group straight from config; every rate is pooled, not an average of per-ad ratios, and carries its n; difference is in-group minus out-group; out-group is the floor set minus the in-group, except H2 whose out-group keeps the child-present precondition (the only non-yes child-present value is no).
- Bootstrap 95% CI of the pooled-ROAS difference resamples ads with replacement within each group separately, 2000 resamples, seed 42, at the primary floor.
- Sign checks (three-floor agreement, median-diff sign, drop-highest-spend ad of each group, leave-one-stop-date-out, product_form strata) and verdict tiers applied exactly as fixed in decision_rules; tier reported before the label cap with tier_after_label_cap kept alongside; label agreement joined from the spot-check as a fact.
- A rerun that empties a group or leaves a ratio undefined is marked not evaluable and excluded from pass/fail; product_form strata report their evaluable (>=5 both sides) and binding (>=10 both sides) counts so a vacuous pass is visible; H8 is tiered on the whole sample per config but flagged as confounded with H6 (every H8 ad is an H6 ad; see S2).
- Structural S1 (three-way child status) and S2 (H8 within H6) reported descriptively at the primary floor; floor-set product_form mix and single-biggest-ad spend share reported for the primary floor.
- Total comparison count reported up front in the performance summary.
- Post-hoc (not pre-registered) descriptive 2x2 of H1 membership x H6 membership at the primary floor, with each cell's pooled ROAS, median per-ad ROAS and pooled hook_rate/ctr/cvr (n beside every rate), plus a product_form split of the H1-out/H6-in cell; motivated by the 22-ad H1/H6 overlap in audit_cross_pair_overlap.csv; descriptive only, no tiers or confidence intervals.
- Post-hoc counts-only composition of the H1-in/H6-out cell at the primary floor: n ads by creator_type and by product_form, with no performance metric attached; descriptive only.
- Creator-scoring weights fixed before any creator is scored in config/weights.yaml: evidence_fit 0.40, audience_response 0.35, sponsored_fit 0.25, each percentile-ranked across the 20 creators; 12-month window anchored to the latest post date; min-data floors with a neutral 0.5 fallback; parent persona, hook_type, ad_format, creator_type and fragile hypotheses given zero weight; hidden-likes, no-audience-data and clinical-persona (legal review) recorded as flags not scores; 200-draw weight-jitter stability (seed 42); at most two clinical-persona creators in the final five; the file is not edited after scoring begins.
- Percentile rank = average rank (ties averaged), computed only among creators with sufficient data for that component, scaled (rank-1)/(m-1) so the lowest sufficient value is 0 and the highest is 1; an insufficient component contributes the neutral 0.5; composite rank ties are broken by creator_id order.
- Creator scoring resolves the 12-month window from the latest post date (2025-10-27 to 2026-10-27 inclusive) and reads all weights, min-data floors, seed, draw count and jitter bounds from config/weights.yaml, with the floors and jitter bounds parsed from the rule strings rather than hand-typed; flags are attached from the config lists and kept separate from each component's computed sufficiency; sponsored_fit excludes is_sponsored=="unclear" posts from both sides; results are ranked by composite with no five selected.
- Post-hoc sensitivity (not pre-registered; frozen creator_scores.csv remains primary), prompted by CR-09's sponsored_fit resting on 3 non-sponsored posts: four alternate composites (frozen; drop sponsored_fit with the other two weights renormalised; sponsored_fit neutral 0.5 for all; sponsored_fit requiring >=4 non-sponsored posts with plays), each creator marked top-5 under all four / some / none.
- Post-sampling uncertainty: 200 within-creator resamples of window posts with replacement (seed 42), recomputing all three components, percentile ranks and the frozen-weight composite, reporting each creator's top-5 share and rank range; scenario C re-ranks the sponsored_fit percentile among the reduced sufficient pool, with evidence_fit and audience_response held at their frozen scores; all scoring logic and parameters are imported from the scoring script, which is not edited.
- Reading pack is presentation only: it reads back the frozen scores, stability and sensitivity outputs and the creator profiles, computes no score and recommends no creators.
- Reading-list inclusion = sensitivity top5_class != "none" OR bootstrap top5_share >= 0.25; the 0.25 threshold is a reading-list rule chosen after seeing the sensitivity results, not a scoring rule, and is a single named constant in the script.
- Reading-pack example posts per creator are chosen by fixed rules (3 most recent children-on-camera-and-product-shown posts; 2 highest (comments+reshares)/plays posts with plays; 2 most recent sponsored posts, fewer if fewer exist), with deterministic tie-breaks, each post shown once with all matching reasons listed; profile text is quoted verbatim from creators.csv.
- Post-hoc validity check (not pre-registered; frozen creator_scores.csv remains primary), prompted by child-and-product example posts that do not show a child reacting to food and by top-engagement posts being "comment for link" posts.
- Link-request posts flagged by one named case-insensitive caption regex (comment ... link/links); the matched snippet for every flagged window post is written out for false-positive review; audience_response is recomputed excluding link-request posts (same median and >=10 posts-with-plays floor, neutral 0.5 otherwise), percentile re-ranked, and the composite recomputed with frozen evidence_fit and sponsored_fit scores and frozen weights; frozen vs new rank reported for all 20.
- Proxy checklist lists the child-and-product example posts (caption excerpt, what_stands_out, post_url) for manual inspection, with no judgement or score attached.
- Post-hoc content scan (not pre-registered), prompted by a hand check finding only 2 of 24 child-and-product example posts show a child eating or reacting to food, so the evidence_fit proxy does not measure feeding content; presentation only, no scores or ranking.
- Food/feeding mentions detected by one named term list matched case-insensitively on word boundaries (so "great" does not match "eat") across topic, caption, what_stands_out, hook_style and brands_visible, over the same 12-month window; per-creator matched share and per-term post counts reported, with matched posts listed for the reading-pack creators plus any creator with >=3 matched posts.
- brands_visible tallied per creator over the window (split on ";"), with post count and sponsored count per distinct brand.
- Post-hoc reading-list additions (not pre-registered; presentation only, no scores/ranking/selection): CR-03, CR-05, CR-20 added by hand after the content scan because child-led and nutrition-authority creators were under-represented, rendered in the same four-section reading-pack layout.
- reading_pack_extra imports has_text and the profile-field and transcript-length constants from the reading-pack script and reproduces its four sections and example-post rules without editing it; the rendering logic is re-implemented because the reading-pack script exposes it only inside main().
- Child-presenter share reported for all 20 creators over the window: posts with presenter_age in {3-7, 8-12, 13-17}, with share and sponsored count, each rate carrying its n.
- Final five chosen by Keshav (human judgement made after the post-hoc validity checks): CR-13, CR-03, CR-11, CR-01, CR-19. In his words: "I distrust the top scores because two of the top five depended on comment-for-link posts and had almost no food content."
- Roles for the five: ad-integrator (CR-13); clinical voices (CR-03 and CR-01); family vlog with a child in every window post (CR-11); milk-category creator (CR-19). Two of the five are clinical/legal-review creators (CR-03, CR-01), so the cap of two clinical creators is respected. Backups in order: CR-05, CR-09, CR-16.
- Frozen top-5 creators not chosen: CR-12 (link-request share 40.0% of 20 window posts; rank 1 unchanged without link-request posts; food-term share 0.0%) and CR-15 (link-request share 45.0%; rank 2 falls to 14 without link-request posts; food-term share 10.0%); figures from link_sensitivity.csv and food_scan.csv.
- CR-20 not chosen: audience is 70.1% men and the creator is an existing Little Joys partner (creator_audience.csv: men_pct 70.1, brands_in_past_year "Little Joys").
- The frozen creator_scores.csv remains the pre-registered primary result; the final five is a separate human judgement and does not alter it.
- Report numbers are produced by src/claims.py into outputs/final/claims.csv (every figure read from files in outputs/ or data/, none typed); outputs/final/manual_checks.csv is the plain record of the post-level manual checks (the 24 proxy checks were made by Keshav; the other rows were AI-assisted and reviewed by Keshav).
